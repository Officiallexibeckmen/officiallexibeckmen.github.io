## 🌟 What Is Zoom-IQA?

Zoom-IQA is a smart tool that automatically evaluates the quality of images. Whether you're a photographer checking your latest shots, a designer reviewing graphics, or someone who just wants to know if a photo is blurry, Zoom-IQA gives you a clear, reliable quality score. It uses advanced artificial intelligence to look at every part of an image—not just the overall picture—so you get honest feedback about which areas are sharp and which ones need work.

## 🎯 Why Use Zoom-IQA?

Regular image quality tools give you one generic score for the whole picture. That's not very helpful. Zoom-IQA goes deeper. It zooms into specific regions of your image and reasons about each area separately. This means you'll know exactly where the problem is—maybe the left side is blurry, or the colors are washed out in the corner. With this detailed analysis, you can fix issues with confidence instead of guessing.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Zoom-IQA free?**
Yes, it's completely free and open source. You can use it for personal or commercial projects without any cost.

**Do I need to install anything else?**
No. Zoom-IQA comes as a standalone application. You don't need Python, MATLAB, or any other software to run it.

**Will it work with RAW images?**
Currently, Zoom-IQA supports common image formats like JPG, PNG, BMP, and TIFF. RAW formats (like CR2 or NEF) are not supported yet, but you can convert them to TIFF first.

**How accurate is the quality score?**
Zoom-IQA uses state-of-the-art AI models trained on thousands of human-rated images. Its scores are very close to what a professional photo editor would judge. However, it's still an automated tool—use it as guidance, not the final word.

**What does "Region-Aware Reasoning" mean?**
Imagine you have a photo of a person standing in front of a blurry background. A simple quality tool would see the whole image as mediocre. Zoom-IQA looks at the person separately and sees they're sharp, while also detecting the background is blurry. It gives you a nuanced report rather than a single average.

**Can I use Zoom-IQA from the command line?**
The primary interface is the graphical app. Advanced users can use a command-line version included in the package for scripting and automation.
